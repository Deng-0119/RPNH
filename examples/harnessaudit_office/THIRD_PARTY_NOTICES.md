# Third-party sources

HarnessAudit is an external dependency, not part of the RPNH kernel. This example
loads the original tasks, Office tools/fixtures and graders from
`UCSB-AI/HarnessAudit@6317162590aeeb1c8dde32b880ac199933343e4a`.
Source: https://github.com/UCSB-AI/HarnessAudit/tree/6317162590aeeb1c8dde32b880ac199933343e4a

No full task/fixture dataset is vendored. Adapter conventions and scoring
integration derive from the original interfaces. This is an independent RPNH
example, not an upstream endorsement or official leaderboard submission.
